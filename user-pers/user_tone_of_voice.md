## Register

- Where you sit between formal and casual, and whether that shifts by audience.
- Punctuation habits worth honouring: em-dashes or not, exclamation marks or not,
  sentence length.

## Never

- Words and constructions you would not use. Be specific; "avoid corporate
  jargon" is not actionable, "never write 'circle back' or 'reach out'" is.

## Samples

Paste two or three things you genuinely wrote. A sample constrains an imitation
far more effectively than a description of one.
