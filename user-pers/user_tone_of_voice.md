## Register

Name the axis and where you sit on it. Too vague: "pretty casual". Usable:
"contractions yes, exclamation marks no, two sentences where most people write
five".

- Where you sit between formal and casual.
- Whether that moves by channel, and how: a pull request comment, an email to a
  client, a message to a colleague.
- Punctuation habits worth honouring: em-dashes or not, exclamation marks or not,
  sentence length.
- Mechanics that give you away: capitalisation (sentence case or title case),
  emoji or none, the greetings and sign-offs you use and the ones you never do.

## Never

- Words and constructions you would not use. Be specific; "avoid corporate
  jargon" is not actionable, "never write 'circle back' or 'reach out'" is.
- Habits to flag as well as words: hedging, apologising, padding a refusal,
  anything that reads as someone else wearing your name.

## Samples

Paste two or three things you genuinely wrote, a paragraph or longer each: a
one-line greeting carries no signal to imitate. If your register moves by
channel, cover the channels you named above, so the claim has something behind
it.

A sample constrains an imitation far more effectively than a description of one.
